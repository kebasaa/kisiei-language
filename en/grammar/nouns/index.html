    The noun¶

    The Kisiei noun consists of a stem and a class suffix. The class determines the plural of the noun and the form of all the words that agree with it.

    • Noun classes: the suffixes, the full form and the stem form;
    • Plural: how to go from singular to plural;
    • Compound nouns: noun + noun, noun + verb, compounds in the Bible;
    • Derivation: suffixes -nɔɔ, -yɛi, -ndɔɔ, prefixes derived from nouns, reduplication.

    The agreement of other words with the noun is described on the Agreement page.
