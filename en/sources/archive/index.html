    Archive¶

    The archive/ folder of the repository keeps older documents on Kisiei: those on which this guide is based, and other sources (word lists, texts, lists of terms) still to be used. Most are scanned typescripts that predate the current spelling: they are not a spelling model, and anything taken from them is checked against the Bible.

    Sékou Sondo Keita and the Académie des langues¶

    Document Contents
    Kissie fondamental (scan) Scan (189 pages) of Sékou Sondo Keita's thesis, Essai d'élaboration d'un kisiei fondamental à l'usage des locuteurs non kisi, Institut polytechnique Gamal Abdel Nasser de Conakry, 1979.
    Kissie fondamental (transcription) Full transcription in Markdown, in the original spelling; the handwritten corrections are incorporated. This transcription is final and will not be converted to the current spelling.
    Kissie fondamental (handwritten notes) Handwritten annotations by later readers, page by page: translations, French glosses, Kissidougou variants (Annotations manuscrites portées sur l'exemplaire numérisé de Keita (1979) s.d.).
    Kissie fondamental (annex) The thesis annex, Contes, proverbes, devinettes (typescript pp. 166–184), found separately and missing from the main scan: 18 pages of Kisiei tales, each followed by proverbs (talelan) and riddles (budon pialan), with handwritten French glosses (Keita 1979). Not transcribed.
    Terminology: language, arts, history, ideology Four lists from the Académie des langues (Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research): numbered French terms with their Kisiei translation, for language and grammar (16 pp.), the arts (12 pp.), history and politics (21 pp.), ideology, economy and society (13 pp.) (Académie des Langues s.d.). The pages name no author; the attribution to Keita comes from the file names.
    Nouvelle orthographe kissi Correspondence sheet between the old and the new spelling (Nouvelle orthographe kissi (tableau de correspondances) s.d.).

    Denise Paulme¶

    Document Contents
    Documents sur la langue kissi Documents sur la langue kissi : lexique et textes (Paulme 1964), 173 pages, printed from the University of Florida Digital Collections. Vocabulary and texts collected by the ethnologist Denise Paulme during two stays in the Kissi country with André Schaeffner (October 1945 – June 1946, October 1948 – May 1949), around Kissidougou and Guéckédou; with a bibliography of earlier work on Kissi.
    Kissi vocabulaire Another copy of the same work (151 pages), scanned from a typescript, with a few handwritten notes.

    Other documents¶

    Document Contents
    Lexique kissi – français Alphabetical Kisiei–French word list of 68 pages, author unknown, typed in the current spelling (ɛ, ɔ), with handwritten additions (Lexique kissi – français s.d.).

    How to cite the 1979 manual¶

    The page numbers cited (“Keita 1979, p. 57”) are those of the typescript. In the transcription, each page begins with a link to the handwritten notes for that page (“✎ Handwritten notes, p. 57”), which makes it easy to find the passage.

    The spelling of quotations from 1979 is that of the original (tyo, nh, è, ö…); the Old and new spelling page explains how to read it.
