# Common phrases

A few common phrases, all taken from the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]]. They show the spelling and the construction; in conversation, polite formulas may vary from region to region.

## Greeting, thanking, asking

| Kisiei | English | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{Pua isɛ}} | Greetings, father. | [Gen 27:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.27.18.BKG) |
| {{Mbaa}} | Yes? (answer to someone calling) | [Gen 27:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.27.18.BKG) |
| {{A yandi}} | Please, I beg you. | [Gen 14:21](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.14.21.BKG) |
| {{balika}} | thank you | [Gen 24:48](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.24.48.BKG) |
| {{Hala dɔw nɔm duaa}} | May God bless you. | [Luke 1:42](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/LUK.1.42.BKG) |
