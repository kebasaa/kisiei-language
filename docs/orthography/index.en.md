# Spelling

This chapter describes the current spelling of Kisiei as practised in the Bible [[(BKG)]]. The rules are drawn from the usage observed in that text (more than 300,000 words) and compared with the 1979 description of the sounds [[(Keita1979, p. 11-19)]].

## The essentials in ten rules

!!! rule "Basic rules"
    1. The alphabet has **26 letters**: *a b c d e ɛ f g h i k l m n ŋ ɲ o ɔ p r s t u v w y*. The letters *j q x z* are not used ([Alphabet](alphabet.md)).
    2. There are **seven vowels**: *a e ɛ i o ɔ u*. A **long** vowel is written **doubled**: {{kolaa}}, {{pɔmbɔɔ}} ([Vowels](vowels.md)).
    3. The sounds [c], [ŋ], [ɲ] are written **c**, **ŋ**, **ɲ** — never *ty*, *nh*, *ny* as in 1979 ([Consonants](consonants.md)).
    4. **kp** and **gb** are single consonants; **mb**, **nd**, **ng** represent prenasalised consonants ({{kpemaa}}, {{pɔmbɔɔ}}, {{ndu}}).
    5. **ŋ** is written at the end of a stem, even before a suffix in *-nd-*: {{naŋ}}, {{lɛŋnden}}; before *g*, write **ng**: {{tenga}}.
    6. **Tones** are marked only on certain words: grammatical words whose tone changes the meaning ({{mí}} / {{mì}}) and a few words that would otherwise be confused ([Tone](tone.md)).
    7. Write a noun in its **full form** when it stands alone ({{cɛiyo}}) and in its **stem form** before a determiner ({{cɛi kon}}) ([Word division](word-division.md)).
    8. The **hyphen** attaches a relative or a class marker to the preceding word at the end of a clause ({{fula-o}}), and joins compounds and reduplicated words ({{ɲɛ-ɲɛ}}, {{taw-taw}}).
    9. Punctuation is as in French, with the quotation marks **« »** and **‹ ›** ([Punctuation and capitals](punctuation.md)).
    10. Write *l* even in the varieties that pronounce *r*; *r* is reserved for proper names and loanwords ([Proper names and loanwords](names-loanwords.md)).
