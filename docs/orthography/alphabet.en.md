# Alphabet

## The letters

The Kisiei alphabet has **26 letters**. The letters specific to Kisiei are **ɛ**, **ɔ**, **ŋ** and **ɲ**; the letters *j*, *q*, *x* and *z* are not used (they do not appear even once in the Bible [[(BKG)]]).

The letter names follow the usage described in 1979 [[(Keita1979, p. 14)]]: each consonant is pronounced followed by a short *e* (*be, de, fe…*) and not the French way (*bé, dé, èf…*).

## Double letters

Several sounds are written with two letters. These are not sequences of two sounds, but a single consonant:

| Group | Sound | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| kp | [k͡p] labial-velar stop | {{kpemaa}} | covenant |
| gb | [ɡ͡b] labial-velar stop | {{langbanɔɔ}} | adult man, elder |
| mb | [ᵐb] prenasalised | {{pɔmbɔɔ}} | small |
| nd | [ⁿd] prenasalised | {{ndu}} | him, her |
| ng | [ᵑɡ] prenasalised | {{tenga}} | shine |
| ll | [lː] long l | {{hangullo}} | heat |

The prenasalised consonants and *kp* are also analysed as single consonants in Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 24, 30-31)]]. The *ll*, on the other hand, is described there as two *l*s belonging to two syllables [[(Childs1995, p. 151)]] (see [Consonants](consonants.md#the-long-consonant-ll)).

Long vowels (*aa, ee, ɛɛ, ii, oo, ɔɔ, uu*) are described on the [Vowels](vowels.md) page.

## Letters with a restricted position

Some letters appear only in certain positions in the word:

- **g** appears only in **ng** and **gb**; no Kisiei word begins with *g*. In Southern Kissi, too, *g* does not exist on its own: an initial *g* in a loanword becomes *k* or *ng* there [[(Childs1995, p. 25, 30)]].
- **w** is written only **after a vowel**, at the end of a syllable: {{taw}}, {{kpow}}, {{nawvo}}. No word begins with *w*.
- **r** appears only in proper names and loanwords: {{Abraham}}, {{rɛsɛn}} “grape”. Some Northern varieties pronounce *r* in ordinary Kisiei words, but the Bible writes *l* in them (see [Regional varieties](../introduction/dialects.md)).
- **ŋ** is found mainly at the end of a stem ({{naŋ}}, {{lɛŋ}}) and before *n* ({{lɛŋnden}}); at the beginning of a word it appears only in a few words, including the numbers {{ŋuɛɛnu}} “five” and {{ŋɔmpum}} “six”.

## Alphabetical order

For sorting words (glossaries, indexes), the following order is proposed, in which each letter specific to Kisiei follows the closest Latin letter:

**a b c d e ɛ f g h i k l m n ŋ ɲ o ɔ p r s t u v w y**

The groups *kp*, *gb*, *mb*, *nd*, *ng* are sorted under their first letter (*kpemaa* under *k*). Tone accents are ignored in sorting.

!!! note "Note"
    This order is a proposal of this guide; neither the 1979 manual nor the Bible gives one. See the [open questions](../reference/open-questions.md).
