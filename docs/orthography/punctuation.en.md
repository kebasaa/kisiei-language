# Punctuation and capitals

## Punctuation

Punctuation follows the rules of French. The Bible [[(BKG)]] uses:

| Sign | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **.** | end of sentence | {{Mí ɲɔɔ tosan lende.}} “And it was so.” [Gen 1:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.9.BKG) |
| **,** | between clauses, in lists | {{Sɛm, Ham a Yafɛt}} “Shem, Ham and Japheth” |
| **:** | before reported speech, a list | {{Mí Hala dimi aa: «…»}} |
| **?** | question | {{Kuɛɛ nɛ n co yɛ?}} “Where are you?” [Gen 3:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.9.BKG) |
| **!** | exclamation, command | {{Má cè laa nɔm!}} “Here is your wife!” [Gen 12:19](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.12.19.BKG) |
| **« »** | reported speech | {{Mó dimi aa: «Diomndo ndu.»}} |
| **‹ ›** | speech reported within other speech | [Gen 3:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.3.BKG) |
| **( )** | clarification, aside | |

### Reported speech

Direct speech is introduced by the verb of speaking followed by {{aa}} “that, this” and a colon, then placed in French quotation marks (guillemets):

{{Mí Hala dimi aa: «Ɲɔ́ɔ́ téngá!» Mí ɲɔɔ tèngà.}} “God said, ‘Let there be light!’ And there was light.” [Gen 1:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.3.BKG)

!!! rule "Rule"
    - Quotation marks are written **« »** (and **‹ ›** inside), with no space inside the quotation marks in the Bible: {{«Diomndo ndu.»}}.
    - The final full stop of the speech goes **before** the closing quotation mark when the quotation is a complete sentence.

!!! note "Spaces"
    French typography puts a space before **: ; ? !**; the Kisiei Bible does not ({{dimi aa:}}, {{yɛ?}}). This guide follows the Bible. Both practices are readable; what matters is to be consistent within a text.

## Capitals

Use a capital letter:

- at the **beginning of a sentence**: {{Mí Hala dimi aa…}};
- for **proper names** of people, places and peoples: {{Abraham}}, {{Esipte}} “Egypt”, {{Yerusalɛm}}, {{Israɛlla}} “the Israelites”;
- for the names of **God**: {{Hala}} “God”, {{Mɛlɛka}} “the Lord”.

The **letters specific to Kisiei** have capitals: **Ɛ, Ɔ, Ŋ, Ɲ** ({{Ɲumma}}, {{Ɔ̀ɔ́}}). Titles and headings in the Bible are sometimes written entirely in capitals (*KANDƆNNDO*).

When a proper name takes a referent with a hyphen, only the first part takes a capital: {{Hala-o}}, {{Aarɔn-nda}}.

## Numbers

Numbers are normally written **in words** in running text ({{pilɛ}}, {{tiɔn}}, {{kɛmɛ}}); figures are used for numbering (chapters, verses, dates). See [Numerals](../grammar/determiners/numerals.md).
