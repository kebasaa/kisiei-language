# Grammar

This part describes the grammar of Kisiei as it emerges from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]], the standard of this guide, comparing it with the 1979 description [[(Keita1979)]] and with the grammar of Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995)]].

The central feature of the language is the **system of noun classes**: every noun belongs to a class, and the class is repeated on the words that go with it. That is why the grammar begins with the noun and agreement.

2. [Determiners and quantifiers](determiners/index.md);
3. [Pronouns](pronouns/index.md);
4. [The verb](verbs/index.md);

6. [The sentence](sentences/index.md).

Each page gives the rule, examples taken from the Bible with their reference, and, in a box, the 1979 usage where it differs.
