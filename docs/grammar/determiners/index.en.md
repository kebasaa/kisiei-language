# Determiners and quantifiers

Determiners are placed **after** the noun, which then takes its stem form; they carry the class suffix of the noun (see [Agreement](../agreement.md)).

- [Adjectives](adjectives.md);
- [Demonstratives](demonstratives.md): *ho*, *kon*, *he*, *ken*…;
- [Possessives](possessives.md): *nuu*, *nɔmndo*, *ndɔɔ*…;
- [Interrogatives](interrogatives.md): *nɛ̀ɛ́*, *yɛɛ*, *kuɛɛ*…;
- [Indefinites](indefinites.md): *pum*, *celen*, *kpow*, *X oo X*…;
- [Numbers](numerals.md): cardinals, ordinals, agreement.
