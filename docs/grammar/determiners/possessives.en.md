# Possessives

The possessive is placed **after** the noun, which takes its stem form: {{cɛi ndɔɔ}} “his house”, {{diola ndɔlan}} “his name”. It varies according to:

- the **person** of the possessor (me, you, him/her, us, you, them);
- the **class** and **number** of the noun possessed;
- and, in the *o* class, according to whether what is possessed is a **person** or a **thing**.

The construction is the same in Southern Kissi: the noun possessed, followed by its class pronoun (except in the *o* class), then the possessive carrying the suffix of the noun [[(Childs1995, p. 106)]].

## Possessed noun of the *o* class: persons and things

The Bible [[(BKG)]] clearly distinguishes two series. With a noun for a **person** (relative, master, servant), the possessive is short; with a noun for a **thing**, it carries the class suffix *-ndo* / *-ɔ*:

| Possessor | Person possessed | Thing possessed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st sing. | {{fuɲa nuu}} “my father” [Gen 20:13](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.20.13.BKG) | {{kɔl nuu}} “my heart” [Gen 6:13](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.6.13.BKG) |
| 2nd sing. | {{fuɲa nɔm}} “your father” [Gen 12:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.12.1.BKG) | {{kɔl nɔmndo}} “your heart” [Josh 14:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/JOS.14.9.BKG) |
| 3rd sing. | {{fuɲa ndɔ}} “his father” [Gen 2:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.24.BKG) | {{cɛi ndɔɔ}} “his house” [Gen 39:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.39.4.BKG) |
| 1st plur. | {{fuɲa naa}} “our father” [Gen 19:34](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.19.34.BKG) | {{cɛi naa}} “our house” [Song 1:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/SNG.1.17.BKG) |
| 2nd plur. | {{fuɲa niaa}} “your father” [Gen 27:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.27.6.BKG) | {{yoomu niaa}} “your life” [Gen 9:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.9.5.BKG) |
| 3rd plur. | {{fuɲa nda}} “their father” [Gen 9:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.9.23.BKG) | {{cɛi ndaa}} “their house” [Neh 3:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NEH.3.23.BKG) |

The short forms {{nɔm}}, {{ndɔ}}, {{nda}} are those of the object [pronouns](../pronouns/personal.md). A few nouns are used with both series depending on the meaning: {{po ndɔ}} “his son” (172 times) but {{po ndɔɔ}} (67 times).

!!! rule "Rule"
    For a relative or a person: {{kala ndɔ}} “his mother” [Gen 2:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.24.BKG), {{laa nɔm}} “your wife” [Gen 3:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.17.BKG). For a thing: {{fonda ndɔɔ}} “his place” [Gen 36:33](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.36.33.BKG), {{kɔl nɔmndo}} “your heart”.

Examples:

- *i*: {{sɔɛi nii}} “my word” [Gen 22:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.22.18.BKG), {{sɔɛi ndɛi}} “their language” [Gen 11:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.11.7.BKG);
- *-on*: {{vanayan nɔmndon}} “your kindred” [Gen 12:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.12.1.BKG);
- *an*: {{yoomuma nian}} “my strength” [Gen 31:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.31.6.BKG), {{kooma ndɔan}} “its blood” [Exod 12:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.12.7.BKG);
- *len*: {{lɛŋnde nilen}} “my land” [Gen 20:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.20.15.BKG), {{lɛŋnde naalen}} “our land” [Gen 26:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.26.16.BKG);
- *lan*: {{silala nilan}} “my laws” [Gen 26:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.26.5.BKG), {{diola ndalan}} “their names” [Gen 10:32](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.10.32.BKG);
- plural persons: {{vanaa nia bɛndua}} “my lords” [Gen 18:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.18.3.BKG), {{poaa ndɔa}} “his sons” [Gen 7:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.7.BKG), {{cuavaa naa}} “our children” [Gen 31:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.31.16.BKG).

In Southern Kissi too, the 1st person singular possessive has two forms, *nù* and *nì*, like {{nuu}} and *ni-* ({{nii}}, {{nilen}}) in the Bible; the possessives there have a low tone, whereas the object pronouns have a high tone [[(Childs1995, p. 106)]].

!!! note "1st sing. with plural persons, and 2nd plur."
    The Bible distinguishes {{nia}} “my” (with plural persons) and {{niaa}} “your” (plural possessor): {{vanaa nia}} “my lords”, {{fuɲaa niaa}} “your fathers”. The 1979 spelling writes both as *niaa* [[(Keita1979, p. 61)]].

## Possessive pronouns

To say “mine, yours…”, the possessive is preceded by the class pronoun [[(Keita1979, p. 60-62)]]: *o nuu* “mine” (*o* class), *i nii* (*i* class), *le nilen* (*len* class), *la nilan* (*lan* class), *ma nian* (*an* class).

## Other ways of expressing possession

Two other constructions are found [[(Keita1979, p. 62-63)]]:

1. with the verb *nɔw* “have” and the particle {{ni}}: *Tamba nɔ cɛiyo hoo ni* “Tamba has this house”;
2. with the class referent and {{ni}}: *Naw Fatee vo ni* “It is Fatee’s cow”.

In the Bible, a named possessor is placed **after** the noun possessed, followed by the class referent: {{lɛŋnde Esipte len}} “the land of Egypt” [Gen 13:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.10.BKG), {{nakɔ Mɛlɛka-o}} “the garden of the Lord” [Gen 13:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.10.BKG).

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 spelling writes the 2nd person *num-* (*nummdo*, *nummde*) [[(Keita1979, p. 61)]]; the Bible writes **nɔm-**: {{nɔmndo}}, {{nɔmnde}}. The 1979 forms of the 1st and 2nd plur. for the *len* and *lan* classes (*nialen*, *nialan*) are {{niaalen}}, {{niaalan}} in the Bible.
