# Qualifying adjectives

## Position and agreement

The qualifying adjective is placed **after** the noun. The noun takes its stem form and the adjective receives the **class suffix** of the noun [[(Keita1979, p. 54-55)]] [[(Childs1995, p. 125-126)]]. The rules and examples below are taken from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]].

| Class | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *o* | {{pee bɛndoo}} | great river [Gen 15:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.15.18.BKG) |
| *o* | {{pee pɔmbɔɔ}} | small river [Gen 15:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.15.18.BKG) |
| plural persons | {{vanaa bɛndua}} | chiefs, masters [Gen 1:28](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.28.BKG) |
| plural persons | {{navaa kɛndɛa}} | fine cows [Gen 41:20](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.41.20.BKG) |
| *i* | {{duɲɛi bɛnduei}} | great feast [Lev 23:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/LEV.23.2.BKG) |
| *i* | {{hoeyii kɛndɛi}} | pleasing smell [Gen 8:21](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.21.BKG) |
| *an* | {{mɛmma bɛnduan}} | great waters, flood [Gen 6:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.6.17.BKG) |
| *len* | {{lɛŋnde kɛndɛlen}} | good land [Deut 8:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/DEU.8.7.BKG) |
| *len* | {{yinnde bɛndulen}} | great fire [Exod 19:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.19.18.BKG) |
| *lan* | {{peela bɛndulan}} / {{peela pɔmbɔlan}} | large / small streams [Exod 7:19](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.7.19.BKG) |
| *lan* | {{kobila bɛndulan}} | great cities [Gen 41:34](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.41.34.BKG) |
| plur. in *-on* | {{cieon bɛndon}} | large villages [Num 32:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NUM.32.16.BKG) |

The noun is put into the stem form described on the [Noun classes](../nouns/noun-classes.md) page: bare stem in the *o* class ({{pee bɛndoo}}), stem + class pronoun in the *an*, *len* and *lan* classes ({{mɛmma bɛnduan}}, {{lɛŋnde kɛndɛlen}}, {{kobila bɛndulan}}). The same order (noun + class pronoun + adjective + suffix) is described for Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 150)]].

## Forms of some common adjectives

The stem of the adjective stays the same; only the suffix changes. Its final vowel combines with the suffix, as in Southern Kissi, where *bɛ̀ndù* + *-ó* gives *bɛ̀ndòó* [[(Childs1995, p. 201)]] (Bible: {{bɛndoo}}):

| Meaning | *o* | plural persons | *i* | *an* | *len* | *lan* | plur. *-on* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| big | {{bɛndoo}} | {{bɛndua}} | {{bɛnduei}} | {{bɛnduan}} | {{bɛndulen}} | {{bɛndulan}} | {{bɛndon}} |
| good, beautiful | {{kɛndɔɔ}} | {{kɛndɛa}} | {{kɛndɛi}} | {{kɛndɛan}} | {{kɛndɛlen}} | {{kɛndɛlan}} | |
| small | {{pɔmbɔɔ}} | {{pɔmbɔa}} | | {{pɔmbɔan}} | {{pɔmbɔlen}} | {{pɔmbɔlan}} | |
| bad | {{vɔɔndo}} | {{vɔɔnda}} | | | | | |

The empty cells correspond to forms not found in the Bible; they are built on the same pattern, with the status <span class="ks-badge ks-res">Respelled</span>.

Adjectives proper form a closed class: dimensions, colours, age, basic qualities [[(Childs1995, p. 126-127)]]. Many other qualities are expressed by **stative verbs** or adjectives derived from verbs; see [Stems and groups](../verbs/stems-and-groups.md).

## The adjective used as a noun

Preceded by the class pronoun, the adjective is used on its own, like a noun: *o humbuo* “the white one (the one that is white)”, *ma tuuvan* “the hot (liquid)” [[(Keita1979, p. 55)]].

## Degree

### Intensity

Intensity is expressed:

- by the adverb {{taw}} “very, a lot” after the adjective or the stative verb: {{dɛnɛ kɛndɔɔ taw}} “a very great good” [Gen 12:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.12.16.BKG), {{n naŋ taw}} “it was very good” [Gen 1:31](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.31.BKG);
- by **reduplication** of the adjective, written with a hyphen: {{bɛndu-bɛnduan}} “very great (waters)” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG).

Intensive reduplication is also described in Southern Kissi (*bɛ̀ndùbɛ́ndù* “very big”) [[(Childs1995, p. 126)]].

### Comparison

Comparison is made with the verb {{hiow}} “surpass” [[(Keita1979, p. 55-56)]]:

- {{Kɛwvo ndóò hiow visia yondoa … kɔlkalu vɔɔndo ni}} “The serpent surpassed all the wild animals in cunning” [Gen 3:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.1.BKG);
- {{pangele hiow vaa}} “the greater light” [Gen 1:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.16.BKG).

Southern Kissi compares in the same way with the verb “surpass”; the superlative there is expressed as “surpass all” [[(Childs1995, p. 283-285)]].

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - The adjective is written **separately** from the noun: {{lɛŋnde kɛndɛlen}}, not *lɛŋndekɛndɛlen*.
    - The class suffix is written only once, on the adjective: {{pee bɛndoo}}, not *peeo bɛndoo*.
    - Fixed compounds are an exception and are written attached or with a hyphen; see [Compound nouns](../nouns/compounds.md).
