# Indefinites

Indefinites refer to beings and things in a vague way: “a certain”, “other”, “all”, “each”, “anyone”. Like the other determiners, they are placed **after** the noun [[(Keita1979, p. 71-73)]]. The forms below are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]].

## Invariable

| Word | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{pum}} | a certain, a | {{Pee bɛndoo pum}} “a great river” [Gen 2:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.10.BKG); {{Telen pum hiovɔɔ}} “some time passed” [Gen 4:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.3.BKG) |
| {{celen}} | other, another | {{po celen}} “another son” [Gen 4:25](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.25.BKG); {{cuavaa celen}} “other children” [Gen 5:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.5.4.BKG) |
| {{tɔ̀a}} | the others | {{vanaa tɔ̀a}} “the other people” [Gen 4:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.15.BKG) |
| {{kpow}} | all, whole | {{o lɛŋnden kpow coo}} “on the whole earth” [Gen 1:11](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.11.BKG); {{súúlan kpow}} “all kinds” [Gen 1:21](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.21.BKG) |
| {{kpede}} | all (without exception) | {{yowva kpede}} “all the birds” [Gen 8:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.17.BKG) |
| {{faw}} | all, entirely | {{visia bɛndua a à pɔmbɔa faw}} “the animals great and small, all of them” [Gen 8:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.17.BKG) |

{{celen}} varies neither in class nor in number ({{po celen}}, {{cuavaa celen}}). {{pum}} corresponds to the Southern Kissi adjective *pùm* “some” [[(Childs1995, p. 127)]].

## “Each”, “any”: *X oo X*

A repeated noun, linked by {{oo}}, means “each, every”:

- {{bil oo bil}} “every plant”; {{yɔm oo yɔm}} “every tree” [Gen 1:29](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.29.BKG);
- {{Visi oo visi}} “each animal” [Gen 2:19](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.19.BKG);
- {{fonda oo fonda}} “everywhere, in every place” [Gen 20:13](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.20.13.BKG).

The noun is in the stem form; {{oo}} is written separately. Several time nouns take a prefix *i-* and other nouns the class pronoun *le-*: {{idii oo idii}} “every morning” [2 Kgs 16:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/2KI.16.15.BKG), {{icɔl oo icɔl}} “every evening” [1 Kgs 17:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1KI.17.6.BKG), {{ipaangu oo ipaangu}} “every month” [1 Kgs 5:28](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1KI.5.28.BKG), {{lepola oo lepola}} “each tribe” [Deut 1:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/DEU.1.23.BKG).

The same construction (the “distributive”) is described for Southern Kissi: a noun repeated around a particle *ó*, with the class pronoun prefixed outside the *o* class, and a prefix *ì-* before certain time nouns [[(Childs1995, p. 197-198)]].

## “Someone, something”: reduplication

Reduplication with a hyphen forms the indefinites for persons and things:

- {{vana-vana}} “someone, anyone”; with negation, “nobody”: {{vana-vana ve lasiɛmbu}} “nobody was ashamed” [Gen 2:25](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.25.BKG);
- {{ɲɛ-ɲɛ}} “something, anything”; with negation, “nothing”: {{ɲɛ-ɲɛ ve lecoo le}} “there was nothing on it” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG).

## “The same, a single”: agreement

{{pilɛ}} “one” forms an indefinite that agrees with the class of the noun, in the sense of “the same, a single”:

| Class | Form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *o* | {{pilɔɔ}} | {{baa pilɔɔ}} “in one place” [Gen 1:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.9.BKG) |
| *i* | {{pilɛi}} | {{dialii pilɛi}} “one flesh” [Gen 2:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.24.BKG) |
| *len* | {{pilɛlen}} | {{Paale pilɛlen}} “the same day” [Gen 17:26](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.17.26.BKG) |
| *lan* | {{pilɛlan}} | {{diomnda pilɛlan}} “the same words” [Gen 11:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.11.1.BKG) |

In the 1979 description, this series is written *pilöö*, *pilèi*, *pilèlen*, *pilèlan* [[(Keita1979, p. 71)]].

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - Indefinites are written separately from the noun: {{po celen}}, {{yowva kpede}}.
    - Reduplicated indefinites take a **hyphen**: {{vana-vana}}, {{ɲɛ-ɲɛ}}.
    - *X oo X* is written as three words: {{visi oo visi}}.

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 description also cites *kèkè* “any” and *yööli* “all” [[(Keita1979, p. 71)]], which do not appear in the Bible; the Bible uses *X* {{oo}} *X* and {{kpow}}. These words remain to be checked with speakers. Southern Kissi has *kɛ́kɛ̀* “any”, preceded by the class pronoun (*ò-kɛ́kɛ̀* “anyone, anything”) [[(Childs1995, p. 160)]].
