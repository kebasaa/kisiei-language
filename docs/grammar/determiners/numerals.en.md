# Numbers

The forms and rules on this page are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]], which contains a great many numbers (ages, censuses, dates). They are compared with the 1979 arithmetic lessons [[(Keita1979, p. 16-63)]] and with the description of Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 112-114)]].

## Cardinal numbers

### From 1 to 10

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | {{pilɛ}} | 6 | {{ŋɔmpum}} |
| 2 | {{tiɔn}} | 7 | {{ŋɔmmɛw}} |
| 3 | {{yaa}} | 8 | {{ŋɔmaa}} |
| 4 | {{hiɔɔlu}} | 9 | {{ŋɔmahiɔɔlu}} |
| 5 | {{ŋuɛɛnu}} | 10 | {{tɔ́}} |

The numbers from 6 to 9 are built on 5: *ŋɔm-* + 1, 2, 3, 4 ({{ŋɔmpum}}, {{ŋɔmmɛw}}, {{ŋɔmaa}}, {{ŋɔmahiɔɔlu}}).

### Tens, hundreds, thousands

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | {{bidiin}} | 70 | {{belaŋɔmmɛw}} |
| 30 | {{beleyaa}} | 80 | {{belaŋɔmaa}} |
| 40 | {{belehiɔɔlu}} | 90 | {{belaŋɔmahiɔɔlu}} |
| 50 | {{beleŋuɛɛnu}} | 100 | {{kɛmɛ}} |
| 60 | {{belaŋɔmpum}} | 1000 | {{vaa}} (plur. {{valan}}) |

The words for 100 and 1000 are loanwords from Manding [[(Childs1995, p. 112)]]. The tens from 30 to 90 are formed with *bele-* / *bela-* + the unit. The hundreds are formed with *le-*: {{kɛmɛ lediin}} “200” [Gen 11:19](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.11.19.BKG), {{kɛmɛ leyáá}} “300” [Gen 5:22](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.5.22.BKG), {{kɛmɛ leŋɔmpum}} “600” [Gen 7:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.6.BKG). Thousands are counted with the plural {{valan}}: {{vàálan tan ŋɔmmɛw a kɛmɛ leŋuɛɛnu}} “7,500” [Num 3:22](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NUM.3.22.BKG).

### Compound numbers

The parts of a compound number are linked by {{a}} “and”, from the largest to the smallest: {{kɛmɛ a beleyaa}} “130” [Gen 5:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.5.3.BKG), {{bidiin a laŋɔmahiɔɔlu}} “29” [Gen 11:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.11.24.BKG).

The **last element** takes a prefix agreeing with the class of the noun counted, as in Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 112-113)]]:

| Noun counted | Prefix | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *lan* ({{vɔsilan}} “years”) | **la-** | {{vɔsilan kɛmɛ leŋɔmahiɔɔlu a tɔ́ a latiɔn}} “912 years” [Gen 5:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.5.8.BKG) |
| persons, animals | **à** | {{visia tɔ́ a à hiɔɔlu}} “14 animals” [Gen 7:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.2.BKG) |
| *len* (ordinal) | **le-** | {{paale bidiin a leŋɔmmɛwndɔle}} “the 27th day” [Gen 8:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.14.BKG) |

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - The elements of a compound number are written **separately**, linked by {{a}}: {{kɛmɛ a beleyaa}}.
    - The agreement prefix is written **attached** to the unit: {{latiɔn}}, {{laŋuɛɛnu}}; the prefix for persons {{à}} is written separately, with its tone.
    - In running text, large numbers may be written in figures, in brackets after the form in words, as the Bible does: “(7 500)”.

## Position of the number and agreement

The number is placed **after** the noun. From 2 upwards, it is often preceded by the **class pronoun** of the noun (object form; see [Personal pronouns](../pronouns/personal.md)):

| Class | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| plural persons | {{simlacia nda yáá}} | three warriors [1 Chr 11:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1CH.11.15.BKG) |
| *i* plur. | {{palɛi ndii yáá}} | three days [1 Chr 12:40](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1CH.12.40.BKG) |
| plur. in *-on* | {{Paangon mún yáá}} | three months [1 Chr 13:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1CH.13.14.BKG) |
| *an* | {{polan man yáá}} | three rows [1 Kgs 6:36](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1KI.6.36.BKG) |
| *lan* | {{kundala bɛndulan tan tiɔn}} | two great (lights) [Gen 1:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.6.BKG) |

Without a pronoun, the number follows the noun directly: {{kɔla palɛi yaa}} “a three days’ journey” [Exod 3:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.3.18.BKG), {{vanaa yaa}} “three people”. The pronoun of the *lan* class takes the form {{tan}} after *n* ({{vàálan tan}}).

“Two” changes form according to the class of the noun counted: {{ŋiɔn}} for persons ({{poaa nda ŋiɔn}} “two sons” [Gen 10:25](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.10.25.BKG)), {{ŋiin}} for the *i* class ({{palɛi ndii ŋiin}} “two days” [Exod 16:29](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.16.29.BKG)), {{miɔn}} for the *an* class ({{polan man miɔn}} “two rows” [1 Kgs 7:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1KI.7.18.BKG)) and {{tiɔn}} for the *lan* class. Southern Kissi has the same series (*ŋìɔ́ɔ́ŋ*, *ŋìíŋ*, *mìɔ̀ɔ́ŋ*, *tìɔ́ɔ́ŋ*), whereas “three” varies little there and “four” not at all [[(Childs1995, p. 113)]]. The class pronoun attaches there to the number (*sálà-láŋ là-tìɔ́ɔ́ŋ* “two sacrifices”) [[(Childs1995, p. 113)]]; in the Bible, it attaches only to the unit of a compound number ({{latiɔn}}).

## Ordinal numbers

“First” is {{tasoo}} [Gen 1:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.1.BKG). The other ordinals are formed with **-ndɔɔ** and agree with the class of the noun:

| Ordinal | *o* class | *i* class | *len* class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| second | {{diinndɔɔ}} | {{diinndɔɛi}} | |
| third | {{yaandɔɔ}} | | |
| fourth | {{hiɔɔlundɔɔ}} | | |
| seventh | {{ŋɔmmɛwndɔɔ}} | | {{ŋɔmmɛwndɔlen}} |
| tenth | {{tɔ́ndɔɔ}} | {{tɔ́ndɔɛi}} | |

Examples: {{paale ŋɔmmɛwndɔlen}} “the seventh day” [Gen 2:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.2.BKG), {{paangii tɔ́ndɔɛi}} “the tenth month” [Gen 8:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.5.BKG), {{paangii diinndɔɛi}} “the second month” [Gen 8:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.8.14.BKG).

“Second” is {{diinndɔɔ}}; {{tiɔnndɔɔ}} is very rare [Neh 13:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NEH.13.6.BKG). The stem *diin* “two” is also found in {{bidiin}} “20” and {{kɛmɛ lediin}} “200”; Southern Kissi likewise forms “second” on *dìíŋ* and says “first” with a separate word, *tásè* [[(Childs1995, p. 112, 201)]].

## Number of times

The prefix **le-** forms adverbs of frequency: {{leyaa}} “three times”, {{leŋɔmmɛw}} “seven times” [[(Keita1979, p. 35)]]. See [Derivation](../nouns/derivation.md).

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 arithmetic lessons give the same numbers in the old spelling (*Pilè, Tiön, Yaa, Hiöölu, Nhuèènu…*, *Kèmè lediin*, *Valan la tiön*) [[(Keita1979, p. 16-63)]]. The Bible writes {{tiɔn}}, {{ŋuɛɛnu}}, {{kɛmɛ}}, and links the class pronoun to the thousands in the form {{vàálan tan}}.
