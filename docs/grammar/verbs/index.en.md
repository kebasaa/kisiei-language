# The verb

The Kisiei verb does not change for person: it is the subject pronoun that shows who is acting. What varies is the **stem** (long or short form, changed vowel), the **tone**, and the small words around it: the auxiliaries {{co}} and {{va}}, the suffix *-nin*, the negative particle {{le}} / {{te}}.

This chapter describes:

- [Stems and groups](stems-and-groups.md): long form and short forms, regular verbs and vowel-alternating verbs;
- [Tense, aspect, mood](tense-aspect-mood.md): perfective, habitual, progressive, future, narrative, subjunctive, imperative;
- [Negation](negation.md): the final particle and the negative stem;
- [Derived verbs](derived-verbs.md): benefactive, middle, reciprocal, causative, plural.

The conjugation tables are in the [Reference](../../reference/verb-tables.md).
