# The noun

The Kisiei noun consists of a **stem** and a **class suffix**. The class determines the plural of the noun and the form of all the words that agree with it.

- [Noun classes](noun-classes.md): the suffixes, the full form and the stem form;
- [Plural](plural.md): how to go from singular to plural;
- [Compound nouns](compounds.md): noun + noun, noun + verb, compounds in the Bible;
- [Derivation](derivation.md): suffixes *-nɔɔ*, *-yɛi*, *-ndɔɔ*, prefixes derived from nouns, reduplication.

The agreement of other words with the noun is described on the [Agreement](../agreement.md) page.
