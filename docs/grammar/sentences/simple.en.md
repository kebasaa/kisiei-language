# The simple sentence

The rules on this page are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]].

## Verbal sentence

The verbal sentence consists of a subject (noun or pronoun), a verb and its objects and complements: {{Mí Kain del Abɛl pɔmbɔ ndɔ coo}} [Gen 4:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.8.BKG). Tense and aspect are carried by the verb, the auxiliary and the pronoun (see [Tense, aspect, mood](../verbs/tense-aspect-mood.md)).

## Sentences of identification and existence

| Construction | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X {{co}} Y {{ni}} | X is Y (identity) | {{Hoo co masa Yuifia ni}} “This is the king of the Jews” [Luke 23:38](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/LUK.23.38.BKG) |
| X {{co}} + place | X is (located) at… | {{Mɛlɛka co o fondaa ho}} “the Lord is in this place” [Gen 28:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.28.16.BKG) |
| X {{va}} + place, state | X was… | {{Lɛŋnden le va tulumasaa}} “the earth was formless” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG) |
| X {{ni}} | it is X | {{Paale taselen le yɔŋnun nden ni}} “that was the first day” [Gen 1:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.5.BKG) |
| X {{co}} … {{le}} | X is not… | {{O co dɛnɛ vana bɛnda le tosa le}} [Gen 20:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.20.9.BKG) |

## Possession: “to have”

“To have” is expressed with the verb {{nɔ}}: {{tɔ́ɔ́ pila ndáà nɔ ni}} “they had strength” [Gen 6:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.6.4.BKG); {{Noe ndóò nɔ vɔsilan kɛmɛ leŋɔmpum}} “Noah was six hundred years old” [Gen 7:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.6.BKG).

## Serial verbs

Two verbs can follow each other to describe a single complex action:

- {{hun a}} “come with” = bring: {{mí Kain hun a ɲɛm}} “Cain brought some produce” [Gen 4:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.3.BKG);
- {{kɔ}} + verb “go and do”: {{Mí Abram kɔ hun a cɔlua kan}} “Abram went and fetched these animals” [Gen 15:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.15.10.BKG); {{N kɔ delnun ndu coo}} “let us go and fall upon him” [Gen 37:20](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.37.20.BKG).

## Reported speech

Speech is introduced by a verb of saying followed by {{aa}} and a colon: {{Mí Hala dimi aa:}} “And God said:” [Gen 1:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.3.BKG); {{mó dimul ndu aa:}} “he said to him:” [Gen 3:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.17.BKG). See [Punctuation](../../orthography/punctuation.md).
