# Word order

The rules on this page are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]] and agree with the description of Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 246-259)]].

## Subject – verb – object

The basic order is **subject + verb + objects and complements**:

| Subject | Verb | Object(s) | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{Hala}} | {{cɔaw}} | {{halataalaa a lɛŋnden}} | “God created the heavens and the earth” [Gen 1:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.1.BKG) |
| {{Kain}} | {{del}} | {{Abɛl pɔmbɔ ndɔ coo}} | “Cain fell upon his brother Abel” [Gen 4:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.8.BKG) |

The object follows the verb directly; then comes the complement of place or manner: {{mó dimul nda aa}} “he said to them” (verb + object + speech) [Gen 1:28](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.28.BKG).

## The resumptive pronoun after the subject

When the subject is a noun of a class other than *o*, it is **taken up again by its class pronoun** just before the verb [[(Childs1995, p. 257-258)]]:

- {{Lɛŋnden le va tulumasaa}} “the earth was formless” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG) (class *len* → {{le}});
- {{Ɲumma bɛndu-bɛnduan ma va matoalaa}} “the great waters were…” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG) (class *an* → {{ma}});
- {{mí vannda a va kɔlan}} “and the people were walking” [Luke 7:12](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/LUK.7.12.BKG) (persons plur. → {{a}}).

With a singular noun of the *o* class, the verb follows directly: {{Mí vanalanɔɔ mulul ndu aa}} “And the woman answered him” [Gen 3:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.2.BKG).

!!! rule "Rule"
    Noun subject (except class *o*) + **class pronoun** + verb: {{Lɛŋnden le va}}, {{kobilan la va}}. The pronoun is written as a separate word.

## Position of other elements

| Element | Position | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Determiners, adjectives, numbers | after the noun | {{pee bɛndoo}}, {{kobio ho}}, {{simlacia nda yáá}} |
| Noun complement (possessor) | after the noun | {{lɛŋnde Esipte len}} “the land of Egypt” [Gen 13:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.10.BKG) |
| Relative clause | after the noun | {{kɔngɔ vesɛi fula-o}} [Gen 1:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.10.BKG) |
| Negation {{le}} / {{te}} | end of clause | {{I sínà le}} [1 Sam 17:55](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1SA.17.55.BKG) |
| Particle {{ni}}, {{yɛ}} | end of clause | {{Paale taselen le yɔŋnun nden ni}} [Gen 1:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.5.BKG) |
| Ideophone | end of clause | {{Diimandɔɔ idiiyo púŋ}} [Gen 21:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.21.14.BKG) |
| Time expression | at the start or end of the sentence | {{O kon kɔɔli, mí…}} “After that…” [Gen 2:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.8.BKG) |

Kisiei thus places the **determiner after the noun** and the **object after the verb**; only the prepositions {{a}}, {{o}}, {{le}} come before their noun, most relations of place being expressed by a postposition ({{o nakɔɔ ho nin}}).

## Focus

To put an element in focus, it is placed **at the start of the sentence** and the sentence ends with the particle {{ni}}: {{Nda yɔŋnun kuubaa cɔvaa masindɛa ni}} “It was they who became the heroes” [Gen 6:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.6.4.BKG). A focused subject takes the independent form of the pronoun ({{Nda}}, {{Ndu}}, {{Ya}}); see [Personal pronouns](../pronouns/personal.md).
