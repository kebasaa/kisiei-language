# Relative clauses

Kisiei has no separate relative pronoun like English *who*, *which*, *that* (French *qui*, *que*). The relative clause is framed by the **class of the noun**: the noun loses its suffix, the relative clause follows, and the class suffix comes back **at the end of the relative clause**, attached by a hyphen to the last word. The rules on this page are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]]; the same structure is described for Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 285-288)]].

## Structure

> **noun (stem form) + [clause] + -class suffix**

| Example (Bible) | Analysis | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{kɔngɔ vesɛi fula-o}} | *kɔngɔ vesɛi* [*fula*]-*o* | the dry land that appeared [Gen 1:10](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.10.BKG) |
| {{mɛmma co lebɛngu-an}} | *mɛm-ma* [*co lebɛngu*]-*an* | the waters that are below [Gen 1:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.7.BKG) |
| {{visia pɔmbɔa kela lɛŋ-nda}} | *visia pɔmbɔa* [*kela lɛŋ*]-*nda* | the small creatures that move on the ground [Gen 1:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.24.BKG) |
| {{sɔɛi Mɛlɛka dimul ndu-e}} | *sɔɛi* [*Mɛlɛka dimul ndu*]-*e* | the word that the Lord had spoken to him [Gen 15:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.15.6.BKG) |
| {{lɔma ya sɔl-lan}} | *lɔma* [*ya sɔl*]-*lan* | the dream that I had [Gen 37:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.37.6.BKG) |
| {{lɛŋnde ya co nɔm cɔm-nden}} | *lɛŋnde* [*ya co nɔm cɔm*]-*nden* | the land that I will show you [Gen 12:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.12.1.BKG) |
| {{ɲɛm vanaa kan ndáà manda le dioo-on}} | *ɲɛm* [*vanaa kan ndáà manda le dioo*]-*on* | the provisions that those people had kept to eat [Gen 14:11](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.14.11.BKG) |

In the *an*, *len* and *lan* classes, the noun keeps its **class pronoun** before the relative clause ({{mɛmma}} = *mɛm-* + *ma*, {{lɛŋnde}}, {{kobila}}), as before an adjective; in the *o* class, the stem is bare ({{kɔngɔ}}). See [Noun classes](../nouns/noun-classes.md).

Often a demonstrative {{hoo}} “this” or {{kon}} “that” is placed at the end of the relative clause, just before the suffix: {{Kpema ya yii hoo-o}} “the covenant that I establish” [Gen 17:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.17.7.BKG), {{fonda n sim kon-ndo}} “the place where you are standing” [Gen 13:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.14.BKG), {{dɛnɛ n tosa hoo-o}} “the thing that you have done” [Gen 3:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.14.BKG). Southern Kissi also uses a demonstrative inside the relative clause, especially when the relativized noun is placed at the start of the sentence as the topic [[(Childs1995, p. 288-289)]].

## The final suffix

The suffix that closes the relative clause is that of the noun’s class; it changes form according to the **last sound of the word** to which it attaches, exactly like noun suffixes [[(Childs1995, p. 151-153)]]:

| Class | after a vowel | after a nasal | after *l* | after *i* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *o* (sing.) | {{-o}}: {{fula-o}} | *-ndo*: {{nin-ndo}} | *-lo*: {{hɔl-lo}} | *-yo*: {{paangei-yo}} |
| plural persons | *-a*: {{coocoo-a}} | *-nda*: {{lɛŋ-nda}} | *-la* | *-ya*: {{ikɛi-ya}} |
| *i* | *-e*: {{ndu-e}} | *-nde*: {{can-nde}} | *-le*: {{del-le}} | *-ye* |
| *an* | *-an*: {{lebɛngu-an}} | *-ndan*: {{nin-ndan}} | *-tan* | *-yan* |
| *len* | *-len* | *-nden*: {{cɔm-nden}} | *-ten*: {{Israɛl-ten}} | |
| *lan* | *-lan* | *-ndan* | *-lan*: {{sɔl-lan}} | |
| plur. in *-on* | *-on*: {{dioo-on}} | *-ndon*: {{mún-ndon}} | *-lon* | *-yon*: {{landei-yon}} |

After *w*, *-o*, *-a* and *-on* become *-vo*, *-va*, *-von* ({{cɔw-von}} [Gen 1:31](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.31.BKG)).

## The subject of the relative clause

In the relative clause, the subject pronouns are not those of the simple sentence ({{i}}, {{a}}, {{o}}…): they form a series of their own, the same as in [questions](../determiners/interrogatives.md).

| Person | Simple sentence | Relative clause, question | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st sing. | {{i}} | {{ya}} | {{lɔma ya sɔl-lan}} “the dream that I had” [Gen 37:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.37.6.BKG) |
| 2nd sing. | {{a}} | {{n}} | {{dɛnɛ n tosa hoo-o}} “the thing that you have done” [Gen 3:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.14.BKG); {{fonda n yeema-o}} “the place that you want” [Gen 13:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.9.BKG) |
| 3rd sing. | {{o}} | {{o}} | {{ɲɛm o cɔw-von}} “the things that he had made” [Gen 1:31](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.31.BKG) |
| 1st plur. | {{n}} | {{naa n}} | {{lɔma naa n sɔl man-ndan}} “the dreams that we had” [Gen 41:12](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.41.12.BKG) |
| 2nd plur. | {{la}} | {{niaa}} | {{lɔma niaa sɔl-lan}} “the dream that you had” [Gen 40:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.40.8.BKG) |
| 3rd plur. | {{a}} | {{ndáà}} | {{ɲɛm ndáà yoo ndu-on}} “the things that they brought him” [Gen 43:26](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.43.26.BKG) |

Note: {{n}} means “you (sing.)” in a relative clause or a question, but “we” in the simple sentence.

When the noun itself is the subject of the relative clause, the verb follows directly: {{kɔngɔ vesɛi fula-o}}.

## Relative clauses of place or time

Nouns of place and time are relativized in the same way:

- {{fonda ni ya va baalaa-o}} “the place where I used to work” [Gen 41:13](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.41.13.BKG);
- {{kobila va o dɛɛla kon nin-ndan}} “the cities that were in that plain” [Gen 13:12](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.12.BKG).

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - The class suffix that closes the relative clause is attached to the **last word** of the relative clause with a **hyphen**: {{fula-o}}, {{sɔl-lan}}, {{cɔm-nden}}.
    - The hyphen is used even if the last word is a postposition ({{coo-o}}, {{nin-ndan}}) or a proper noun ({{Israɛl-ten}}).
    - Do not write the suffix as a separate word.

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 description presents these suffixes as separate **relative pronouns**, placed after the verb: *vo, ve, van, len, lan*, plural *va, von* [[(Keita1979, p. 88-89)]] (*Domaa n pendu vo sèsa* “the shirt that you bought is beautiful”). The Bible attaches them to the last word of the relative clause with a hyphen; this usage is authoritative. See [Word division](../../orthography/word-division.md).
