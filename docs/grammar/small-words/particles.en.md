# Particles

Particles are small words with no lexical meaning of their own, which mark a grammatical relation or a nuance. The forms and examples below are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]], compared with the 1979 description [[(Keita1979, p. 117-118)]] and with that of Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 142-145)]].

## Sentence particles

| Particle | Function | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{ni}} | affirmation, identification, focus (“it is…”) | {{Paale taselen le yɔŋnun nden ni}} “That was the first day” [Gen 1:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.5.BKG); {{Hoo co masa Yuifia ni}} “This is the king of the Jews” [Luke 23:38](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/LUK.23.38.BKG) |
| {{le}} / {{te}} | negation, at the end of the clause | see [Negation](../verbs/negation.md) |
| {{yɛ}} | ends a content question, after the verb | {{Kuɛɛ nɛ n co yɛ}} “Where are you?” [Gen 3:9](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.9.BKG); see [Interrogatives](../determiners/interrogatives.md) |
| {{aa}} | introduces reported speech | {{Mí Hala dimi aa}} “And God said:” [Gen 1:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.3.BKG) |
| {{pɛ}} | closes the condition opened by {{te}} | {{te o de pɛ kɔmnde yɔm kon}} [Gen 3:22](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.22.BKG) |

The particle {{ni}} ends the sentence that identifies or focuses an element; in the corresponding negative sentence, it is replaced by the final negation (see [Negation](../verbs/negation.md)). Southern Kissi has the same focus particle *ní* [[(Childs1995, p. 124)]].

## Politeness particle: *-ndɔ*, *-yɔ*, *-lɔ*

Attached to the verb of an order or a request, this particle softens the demand (“then, please”); it is written **with a hyphen** and takes the form *-ndɔ* after a nasal, *-yɔ* after *i*, *-lɔ* after *l*:

- {{La yaŋ-ndɔ nilan}} “Listen to me, then” [Gen 4:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.23.BKG);
- {{la tuei-yɔ ɲɛ ya co niaa dimul-lo}} “hear what I am telling you” [Gen 4:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.23.BKG);
- {{La mɛi-yɔ}} “Wait, please” [Gen 18:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.18.5.BKG).

Southern Kissi has the same politeness particle (*wɔ*, variants *ndɔ*, *lɔ*) after the imperative and the hortative [[(Childs1995, p. 144)]]. The Bible also uses *-lɔ* in other contexts ({{ndóò vil-lɔ … le}} [Gen 2:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.5.BKG)), whose meaning remains to be clarified (see the [open questions](../../reference/open-questions.md)).

## Noun phrase particles

Placed just after the noun or pronoun, they specify its scope [[(Keita1979, p. 117-118)]]:

| Particle | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{pila}} | self (himself) | {{Niaa pila bɛɛ la sina}} “you yourselves know” [Gen 31:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.31.6.BKG) |
| {{bɛɛ}} | also; even | {{yɔmnde bɛɛ fulá}} “let the trees also come forth” [Gen 1:11](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.11.BKG) |
| {{kpé}} | only, alone | {{nɔm pilɛ kpé}} “you alone” [Gen 7:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.1.BKG) |
| {{kpeekpei}} | exactly, precisely | {{fonda … kpeekpei}} “the very place” [Gen 13:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.13.4.BKG) |
| {{daama}} | only | {{latámán kɛmɛ lehiɔɔlu daama}} “only four hundred (shekels)” [Gen 23:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.23.15.BKG) |
| {{vɛlɛ}} | again, also (after the verb) | {{Mí Hala dimi vɛlɛ aa}} “God said again” [Gen 1:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.6.BKG) |

{{vɛlɛ}} is the only one of these particles that is placed after the verb [[(Keita1979, p. 118)]].

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - Particles are written **separately**: {{ya pila}}, {{nɔm pilɛ kpé}}, {{dimi vɛlɛ}}.
    - Only the politeness particle is attached to the verb, with a **hyphen**: {{yaŋ-ndɔ}}, {{tuei-yɔ}}.

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 description also cites *kuani*, *tuun* and *sii* “only” and *pɛn* [[(Keita1979, p. 117)]], which do not appear, or hardly appear, in the Bible.
