# Interjections, greetings and responses

Interjections express an emotion, a call or a response; they are set off from the rest of the sentence by a comma or an exclamation mark. The forms below are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]].

| Word | Use | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{Oi}} | yes | {{Oi, i yeema}} “Yes, I will” [Gen 24:58](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.24.58.BKG) |
| {{Ɛ-ɛ}} | no | {{Ɛ-ɛ, n nɔla mì n de}} “No, we may eat” [Gen 3:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.2.BKG) |
| {{Mbaa}} | answer to a call (“yes?”) | {{Mbaa. Nɔm nɛ̀ɛ́-nɛ̀?}} “Yes? Who are you?” [Gen 27:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.27.18.BKG) |
| {{isɛ}} | greeting | {{Pua isɛ}} “Greetings, father” [Gen 27:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.27.18.BKG) |
| {{balika}} | thank you | {{mí mel ndu balika}} “I thanked him” [Gen 24:48](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.24.48.BKG) |
| {{Ɛ̀ɛ̀}} | astonishment, admiration | {{Ɛ̀ɛ̀ hɛ́ɛ̀!}} “Ah! Here at last…” [Gen 2:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.23.BKG) |
| {{Yàá}} | surprise | {{Yàá Mɛlɛka co o fondaa ho!}} “Surely the Lord is in this place!” [Gen 28:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.28.16.BKG) |

Southern Kissi adds to greetings an emphatic particle *ó* (*ìsɛ̀ó*, *ìdììyó*), which makes them warmer [[(Childs1995, p. 144-145)]]. In the Bible, {{idii}} means “in the morning, early” ({{Siɔdii idii}} “tomorrow morning” [Gen 19:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.19.2.BKG); {{idii a icɔl}} “morning and evening” [Exod 27:20](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.27.20.BKG)); its use as a leave-taking formula still needs to be checked.

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - A reduplicated interjection takes a hyphen: {{Ɛ-ɛ}}.
    - The tone marks on interjections ({{Ɛ̀ɛ̀}}, {{Yàá}}) are written as in the Bible.
    - The greeting is written as two words: {{Pua isɛ}}.

!!! keita "1979 usage"
    The 1979 description cites *kanikokoe* (joy), *se* (call), *kpoki* (surprise), *kpöö* (disgust) and *taa* [[(Keita1979, p. 119)]]; these forms do not appear in the Bible.
