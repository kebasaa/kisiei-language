# Prepositions, postpositions and conjunctions

The forms and examples on this page are drawn from the texts of the Kisiei Bible [[(BKG)]]; they are compared with the 1979 description [[(Keita1979, p. 117-119)]] and with that of Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 129-131, 141-142)]].

## Prepositions

Kisiei has only three simple prepositions, like Southern Kissi [[(Childs1995, p. 129)]]:

| Preposition | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{a}} | with, by means of; and | {{vanaa bɛndua a à pɔmbɔa}} “great and small”; {{cɔwnun a yɔmnde}} “made of wood” [Deut 4:28](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/DEU.4.28.BKG) |
| {{o}} | at, in, on (place, time) | {{o paale ŋɔmmɛwndɔlen coo}} “on the seventh day” [Gen 2:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.2.BKG) |
| {{le}} | for, because of | {{le dioo}} “to eat” [Gen 3:11](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.11.BKG); {{Le hee}} “because of that” [Gen 2:21](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.21.BKG) |

Watch out for homonyms: {{o}} the preposition and {{o}} the pronoun “he, she”; {{le}} “for” and {{le}} the negative particle; {{a}} “with, and” and {{a}} the pronoun “you, they”.

## Circumpositions: *o* … postposition

To specify a location, the noun is framed by {{o}} and a **postposition** placed after it [[(Childs1995, p. 129-131)]]:

| Construction | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{o}} … {{nin}} | in | {{o nakɔɔ ho nin}} “in this garden” [Gen 2:16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.16.BKG) |
| {{o}} … {{coo}} | on | {{o mɛnndan coo}} “on the waters” [Gen 1:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.2.BKG) |
| {{o}} … {{lo}} | at the home of, to (a person) | {{o vanapɔnɔɔ lo}} “to the man” [Gen 2:19](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.19.BKG) |
| {{o}} … {{ba bɛngu}} | in the hands of, by | {{Mɛlɛka o ba bɛngu}} “by the Lord, from the hand of the Lord” [Gen 4:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.4.1.BKG) |
| … {{tɛɛn}} | between, among | {{mɛnndan sɛiyo tɛɛn}} “in the midst of the waters” [Gen 1:6](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.6.BKG) |
| … {{ikɛi}} | with, beside | {{Noe ikɛi}} “to Noah” [Gen 7:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.7.15.BKG) |
| … {{kɔɔli}} | after, behind | {{O kon kɔɔli}} “after that” [Gen 2:8](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.8.BKG) |

The 1979 description likewise gives *o … lo* “at the home of” and *o … nin* “in” [[(Keita1979, p. 119)]].

!!! rule "Spelling rule"
    {{o}} and the postposition are written as separate words: {{o nakɔɔ ho nin}}, {{o mɛnndan coo}}. When a relative clause ends with the postposition, the class ending is attached to it with a hyphen: {{coo-o}}, {{nin-ndan}} (see [Relative clauses](../pronouns/relative.md)).

## Coordinating conjunctions

| Word | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{a}} | and (between nouns) | {{halataalaa a lɛŋnden}} “the heavens and the earth” [Gen 1:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.1.BKG) |
| {{nda}} | and (between names of people) | {{Moise nda Aarɔn}} “Moses and Aaron” [Exod 5:1](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/EXO.5.1.BKG) |
| {{kɛ}} | but | {{Kɛ mí Mɛlɛka mulul ndu aa}} “But the Lord answered him” [Num 12:14](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NUM.12.14.BKG) |
| {{ɔ́ɔ̀}} | or (else), is it that | {{Kɛ ɔ́ɔ̀ saa téndé…}} “but where is the lamb…?” [Gen 22:7](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.22.7.BKG) |
| {{te kon te}} | or else, otherwise | {{te kon te}} [Gen 24:21](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.24.21.BKG) |
| {{mi}} | and (then); see [Tense, aspect, mood](../verbs/tense-aspect-mood.md) | {{Mí Hala dimi aa}} [Gen 1:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.3.BKG) |
| {{lelan}} | that is why, therefore | {{Lelan, vanapɔnɔɔ faŋannin fuɲa ndɔ}} “That is why a man will leave his father” [Gen 2:24](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.24.BKG) |
| {{nduyɛ}} | however, indeed | {{Kɛ nduyɛ, la dia vision…}} “Only, you shall not eat the flesh…” [Gen 9:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.9.4.BKG) |

{{nda}} links names of people or animals, {{a}} other nouns [[(Keita1979, p. 119)]]: {{Mí Bɛnyamɛn nda Hakub va baalaa tosaa}} “Benjamin and Hasshub were working” [Neh 3:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/NEH.3.23.BKG).

## Subordinating conjunctions

| Word | Meaning | Example (Bible) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{kani}} | because, for | {{kani o falun…}} “for she was taken…” [Gen 2:23](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.23.BKG) |
| {{le tùú}} | because, since | {{Le tùú n cɛl sɔɛi laa nɔm-nde}} “Because you listened to your wife” [Gen 3:17](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.17.BKG) |
| {{haliko}} | so that | {{haliko mi la va baloo dɔwnɔnndo}} “so that it may be your food” [Gen 1:29](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.29.BKG) |
| {{te}} … {{pɛ}} | if, when | {{te o de pɛ kɔmnde yɔm kon}} “if he eats of the fruit of this tree” [Gen 3:22](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.3.22.BKG) |
| {{mɛɛ}} … {{yɛ}} | as, in the way that | {{mɛɛ súúla ndɔlan la co yɛ}} “according to their kinds” [Gen 1:11](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.11.BKG) |
| {{Mɛɛ}} | when (narrative) | {{Mɛɛ Hala cii baala kon tosa kpow}} “When God had finished his work” [Gen 2:2](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.2.BKG) |
| {{Tuma}} | at the time when, when | {{Tuma Mɛlɛka Hala cɔw halataalaa a lɛŋnden-ndo}} “When the Lord God made the heavens and the earth” [Gen 2:4](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.4.BKG) |
| {{maa}} | that (after say, see, know) | {{mó ca maa n naŋ taw}} “he saw that it was very good” [Gen 1:31](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.31.BKG) |
| {{aa}} | (introduces speech) | {{Mí Hala dimi aa: «…»}} [Gen 1:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.1.3.BKG) |
| {{háá}} | until, up to | {{háá o kpóŋndán}} “up to the ceiling” [1 Kgs 6:15](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/1KI.6.15.BKG) |
| {{hali}} | even; even if | {{hali billo bɛɛ ndóò vil-lɔ le}} “not even the plants had sprung up yet” [Gen 2:5](https://www.bible.com/bible/1394/GEN.2.5.BKG) |

The condition is built with {{te}} at the start and {{pɛ}} **after the verb** of the clause: {{Te … pɛ}}. Southern Kissi uses the same construction (*tè … pɛ́*), as well as *àà* “that”, *màà* “that, as”, *mɛ̀ɛ́ … (yɛ́)* “when, as” [[(Childs1995, p. 141-142)]]. The 1979 description also cites *hanikpe* “so that”, *baw* and *fuule* “because” [[(Keita1979, p. 119)]], which are absent or very rare in the Bible.

!!! rule "Spelling rules"
    - {{aa}} introducing speech is followed by a **colon** and quotation marks: {{dimi aa: «…»}} (see [Punctuation](../../orthography/punctuation.md)).
    - {{te}} “if” opens the clause and {{pɛ}} follows its verb; do not confuse them with the final negation {{te}}.
    - {{háá}} “until” carries its accents, to distinguish it from {{haa}} “these”.
