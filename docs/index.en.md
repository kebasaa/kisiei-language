# Kisiei — spelling and grammar

A reference guide for writing and teaching Kisiei (Northern Kissi).

</div></div>

This guide is for everyone who **writes**, **corrects** or **teaches** Kisiei: teachers, translators, literacy workers, authors. It answers two questions: *how do you spell this word?* and *how does this construction work?*

- [Introduction](introduction/index.md): the language, its regional varieties, the history of its writing and the sources of the guide.
- [Spelling](orthography/index.md): the alphabet, vowels, consonants, tones, word division and punctuation, with a [table from the old to the new spelling](orthography/old-and-new.md).
- [Grammar](grammar/index.md): the noun and its classes, agreement, determiners, pronouns, the verb, invariable words and the sentence.
- [Reference](reference/index.md): verb tables, word lists by topic, common phrases, a glossary and [open questions](reference/open-questions.md).

## The standard: the Kisiei Bible

The spelling in this guide is that of the **Kisiei Bible** (*Bii Bila*) [[(BKG)]]. It is the longest and most recent text published in the current spelling; when another source spells a word differently, the Bible's form is authoritative. The grammar is based on Sékou Sondo Keita's manual ([[Keita1979|1979]]), reread in the light of the Bible's usage: every rule from 1979 has been checked against the biblical text, and the examples are taken from it wherever possible.

## Reading the examples

Kisiei words are in italics and underlined. The colour of the underline shows where the form comes from (details appear when you hover over it):
